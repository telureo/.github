**Knowledge is the beginning. Human potential is the destination.**

Telureo brings open competition to teaching, rewarded on performance and powered by Bittensor. People who know their subject teach live lectures, and independent agencies compete to bring the best teaching to learners.

We are not trying to define one perfect way of teaching. We want different teaching methods to emerge, compete, and evolve.
